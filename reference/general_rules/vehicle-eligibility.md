# Who may not park under an ordinary P

> A general traffic rule. **It is not on the sign** — check it yourself. It never enters the engine's computation.

A lone `P` permits parking for all registered motor vehicles, but whatever the sign says,
these may not park there:

- **an unhitched trailer or a caravan** — unless a plate permits them outright;
- **an unregistered or deregistered** vehicle;
- **a bicycle or a class II moped** — by the rules these count as cycles, and they need
  cycle parking;
- a vehicle that **does not fit entirely within the markings**: overhanging the next
  space or the carriageway means a fine.
