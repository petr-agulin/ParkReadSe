# No parking (C35)
Primary road sign. Category - "Prohibitary sign".
The sign prohibits the parking of vehicles. The indication applies on the side of the road where the mark is erected. The indication begins to apply where the mark has been erected and applies as stated on an additional board, to the place where another provision on stopping or parking is marked, to the next intersection with a road other than that referred to in ch. § 21 second paragraph of the Traffic Ordinance (1998: 1276] or to the place before that where a corresponding mark with additional sign T11, extent, with downward arrow, has been set up. The statement also applies again without the sign being re-erected in the event that a parking space has been arranged next to the carriageway. or listed on an additional board.

# No parking on odd-numbered dates (C36)
Primary road sign. Category - "Prohibitary sign".
The more detailed provisions relating to sign C35, Vehicle parking is prohibited on odd-numbered dates.

# No parking on even-numbered dates (C37)
Primary road sign. Category - "Prohibitary sign".
The more detailed provisions relating to sign C35 - Vehicle parking is prohibited, on even-numbered dates. 

# Date parking (C38)
Primary road sign. Category - "Prohibitary sign".
The sign indicates that it is prohibited to park vehicles on days with even-numbered dates on the side of the road that has even-numbered addresses and on days with odd-numbered dates on the side of the road that has odd-numbered addresses.

# No stopping or parking (C39)
Primary road sign. Category - "Prohibitary sign".
The sign indicates it is prohibited to stop or park a vehicle. The more detailed provisions relating to sign C35 - Vehicle parking is prohibited.

# Special rules for stopping and parking (C45)
Primary road sign. Category - "Prohibitary sign".
The sign indicates the prohibition indicated by the inserted sign. The sign may also indicate a deviation from the regulation indicated by the sign under which it is affixed. Other prohibitions can be indicated by the sign. The Swedish Transport Agency may issue regulations on which other prohibition signs may be inserted. The sign is posted under one of the signs C35, prohibition on parking vehicles, C36, prohibition on parking vehicles on day with odd dates, C37, prohibition on parking vehicles on day with even date, C38, date parking, C39, prohibition on stop and park vehicles, C40, purpose location, C42, turning area, D10, mandatory lane or lane for vehicles in scheduled traffic, etc., E19, parking or E20, area mark. Sign E20, area mark, may be inserted. If the sign is inserted, the more detailed regulations for sign E20, area mark, apply. For time, the more detailed regulations for additional board T6, time indication apply. Other restrictions can only be indicated on the sign.

# Parking (E19)
Primary road sign. Category - "Instruction sign".
The sign indicates that parking is permitted in a parking lot or on a stretch on the side of the road where the road sign is posted.

# Zone sign (E20)
Primary road sign. Category - "Instruction sign".
The sign is erected at the entrances to the area and applies up to the point where the sign E21, end of zone, is erected or it is otherwise clearly indicated that the instructions on the sign no longer apply. Within the area, there may be deviations from the instructions on the sign. These deviations are indicated through other road signs and additional panels. The instructions on the sign also apply after such deviation without the sign being repeated.

# Zone ends (E21)
Primary road sign. Category - "Instruction sign".
The sign indicates that an area with prohibitions or permits indicated with a sign E20, area mark, ceases. The appearance is adapted to the sign inserted in mark E20, area mark.

# Special rules for parking (E30)
Primary road sign. Category - "Instruction sign".
The panel indicates a deviation from the sign under which it is placed or from Chapter 3, Section 53, point 10 or Section 54 of the Road Traffic Ordinance (1998:1276). It is used beneath one of the following signs; C35, Vehicle parking is prohibited, C36, Vehicle parking is prohibited on odd-numbered dates, C37, Vehicle parking is prohibited on even-numbered dates, C38, Date parking, C39, No stopping or parking, C40, Purposeful location, C42, Turning area, D10, Reserved lane or carriageway for vehicles operating a regular service etc., E19, Parking, E20, No parking zone, or E22, Bus stop. One of these signs is depicted in miniature on the panel. For the times, the more detailed provisions relating to panel T6, Time indication, apply. The background colour on the panel is the same as the background colour on the sign depicted on the panel according to what is stated in Section 29.

# Parking facility (F28)
Primary road sign. Category - "Direction sign".
Information indicating that the parking location is full or has spaces available can be provided adjacent to the sign. The name of the parking facility may be included in the sign. The sign may be incorporated in a location sign for direction. No other colour may be used.

# Park and ride (F29)
Primary road sign. Category - "Direction sign".
The sign indicates parking space in connection with public transport. The symbol on the sign indicates the type of public transport available. The Swedish Transport Agency may issue regulations on which other symbols may be used. The name of the car park may be inserted in the sign. Deviation from color must not occur.

# Time indication (T6)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The panel indicates when the indication on the road sign applies. If times are used in combination with other conditions, the panel also indicates when these conditions apply. Black or white figures without brackets indicate weekdays, except weekdays before Sundays or public holidays. Black or white figures within brackets indicate weekdays before Sundays or public holidays. Red figures indicate Sundays or public holidays. If the time limit extends past midnight, it applies after midnight the following day. If a certain day or certain weekday is indicated, the regulation applies to this day regardless of whether the day is a weekday, Sunday or public holiday.

# Disabled (T7)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The board states that the indication on the mark applies to those who are allowed to park according to a parking permit referred to in ch. Section 8 or 9 of the Traffic Ordinance. The board is only used under brand E19, parking. If time is indicated with board T6, time indication, the supplementary instructions apply in accordance with the detailed regulations for board T6, time indication.

# Symbol panel (T8)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The sign indicates that the mark under which the sign is affixed only applies to the type of vehicle or group of road users indicated on the sign. The vehicle type or road user group is indicated with a symbol in accordance with section 28. If time is stated according to board T6, time indication, the supplementary instruction according to the detailed regulations for board T6, time indication applies.
Includes:

- T8-5 - Bus
- T8-6 - Tractor / Slow-moving vehicle (Agricultural tractors and working machinery)
- T8-7 - Motorcycle
- T8-8 - Bicycle / Class II moped
- T8-9 - board trailer 
- T8-10 -  Pedestrians
- T8-11 - Persons with disabilities (Disabled drivers/passengers)

# Extent (T11)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The panel with arrows pointing in two directions, placed horizontally or vertically, means that the indication applies to both sides of the sign. The panel with the arrow pointing down means that the indication ends there. The panel with a right or left-pointing arrow means that the indication applies in the direction to which it points. If the panel is used under an E19 sign, parking, the black symbol is on a white background with no border. The panels can also be used independently to demarcate parking spaces in an area, if there is a sign at the entrance to the area stating that parking is permitted.
Includes: 
- T11-2 - Indicates the restriction ends at the sign or applies towards the reader.
- T11-3 - Applies to the left
- T11-4 - Applies to the left (White background variant, used for general direction).
- T11-5 - Applies to the right (White background variant, used for general direction).
- T11-6 - Applies in both directions (left and right) (Yellow background with red border, used with warning or prohibition signs).
- T11-7 - Applies in both directions (forward and backward) (Yellow/red variant indicating the restriction continues on both sides of the sign).
- T11-8 - Ends at the sign / Applies backwards (Yellow/red variant pointing down, showing where a restriction finishes).
- T11-9 - Applies to the left (Yellow background with red border, used with prohibition/warning signs).
- T11-10 - Applies to the right (Yellow background with red border, used with prohibition/warning signs).

# Fee (T16)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The board states that a fee must be paid for parking according to the rate stated on a parking meter, ticket machine or equivalent and that other conditions for parking stated there must be observed. If time is stated according to board T6, time indication, the supplementary instruction according to the detailed regulations for board T6, time indication applies.

# Parking disc (T17)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The panel indicates that the time when parking begins shall be stated using a parking disc or some other device. For the times, the more detailed provisions relating to panel T6, Time indication, apply.

# Duration of parking permitted (T18)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The board indicates the maximum time allowed for parking. If time is stated according to board T6, time indication, the supplementary instruction according to the detailed regulations for board T6, time indication applies.

# Residents (T19)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The board states that residents in an area, with a special permit, may park according to special conditions. The text can be supplemented with an area designation or equivalent. If time is stated according to board T6, time indication, the supplementary instruction according to the detailed regulations for board T6, time indication applies.

# Parking ticket (T20)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The board states that parking is free of charge but that a parking ticket must be used for parking. If time is stated according to board T6, time indication, the supplementary instruction according to the detailed regulations for board T6, time indication applies.

# Positioning of vehicles (T21)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The panel indicates how a vehicle is to be positioned in a parking space. The panel is specific to the conditions at the location. Perpendicular to the edge of the road.
Also includes: 
- T21-2 - parallel to the edge of the road
- T21-3 - diagonally to the edge of the road

# Text (T22)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The board provides additional instructions that cannot be given with any other additional board or combinations of these. If time is stated according to board T6, time indication, the supplementary instruction according to the detailed regulations for board T6, time indication applies.

# Charging station (T24)
Road sign of category - "Additional panel (plate)".
The board indicates a location for external charging of electricity for vehicle propulsion. The board is used under mark E19, parking, and states that only vehicles with the possibility of external charging of electricity for the vehicle's propulsion may park.
