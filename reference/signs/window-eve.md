The *vardag före sön- och helgdag* class — a working day before a red day. Usually a
Saturday, but in 2026 there are nine such weekday eves: for example, 30 April before the
First of May.

Saturday gets brackets not as a day off: in Swedish terms Saturday is a working day, and
it gets brackets as the day before a Sunday.
