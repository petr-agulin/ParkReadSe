**It does not narrow who may park.** It says that people living in the area may have
special terms.

Hence the reading rule: someone who is not a resident reads the sign as if this plate
were not there at all; that is the product's main scenario. A resident of the area is
outside the product's scope: their terms are agreed with the property manager, differ
from building to building and are not written on the sign.

What follows `Boende` is usually the area's name or code — `Solna`, `GK-J`, `C-NV`, `C4n` —
and it says which residents, not who else. It is read as part of the name, on the same
line or on its own. A plate that says more than who — `Endast` (only), `Ej`, `Utom`,
`Övriga`, hours, a fee, a length of stay — is not read away like that: whatever on it
was not understood stays in sight, because there it may be a rule for everyone.
