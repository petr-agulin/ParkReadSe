Only two-wheeled motorcycles and class I mopeds. Not cars, not motorcycles with a
sidecar, not trikes or quad bikes.

The answer for such a sign gives **the full regime for motorcycles**, not the phrase "you
may not park here": the product does not know what the reader arrived in.
