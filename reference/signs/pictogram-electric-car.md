Electric cars **and plug-in hybrids** — the developer's reading of photograph `022`.
This must not be reduced to "electric cars": a hybrid that is charging meets the
condition.

The word `laddande` matters just as much: the condition is not "to be an electric car"
but **to be charging**. It narrows the group and at the same time sets a requirement on
the state of the car, so the answer names it in full rather than shortening it to "for
electric cars".
