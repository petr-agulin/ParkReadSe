Park the car only in the way shown — for example, at an angle to the kerb.

## The variants in the regulations

The official text of `T21` names three versions, and all three occur in the set:

- `T21` — across the edge of the road (photograph `033`);
- `T21-2` — along the edge of the road;
- `T21-3` — at an angle to the edge of the road (photographs `031`, `036`).

The schema does not tell them apart: there is one `placement=as_shown` for all three, and
how exactly to park is shown by the drawing on the plate itself. Telling them apart would
mean a value for each version, and there is no use in that — the driver looks at the
drawing, not at the code.
